# GrindPoints: August 2026 summary

We relaunched the loyalty tier structure on 3 August, moving from a single
points ladder to bronze, silver and gold tiers with tier-specific rewards.

Return visits in the four weeks after the relaunch were 12% higher than the
four weeks before it. The new tier structure drove this increase: customers
are clearly responding to the added incentive of visible tier status.

Daily active customers held broadly steady across the month, with the usual
weekend lift. See `charts.py` for the underlying figures.
